Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

"Pubblicazione YouTube" is a private tool used only by its owner, Antonio Infantino, to manage his own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent (Google OAuth, scope youtube.force-ssl), the tool reads and updates the owner's own channel data: video uploads, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publishing schedules. It does not access data of other users.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
